# Tutorial video manager

In Admin, open Video Manager, choose an audience and select its welcome video or a feature. Paste a YouTube watch, share, Shorts, live or embed link, or the iframe copied from YouTube. Select **Preview link** and play the video to check that embedding is enabled. Select **Save draft**, then **Publish video** and confirm.

The current guide video stays live while you preview or save a replacement. Discard removes the draft. Unpublish removes the live video and keeps the existing draft, or moves the published video to draft if there is no replacement.

Aim4price stores a canonical YouTube embed URL. YouTube hosts playback and controls video visibility. The guide retains its existing audience access rules. Previously uploaded recordings still play through the authenticated media endpoint.
