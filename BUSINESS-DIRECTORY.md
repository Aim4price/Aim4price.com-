# Business accounts and external sharing

Admins create accounts under **Accounts → Add account**. Businesses can also sign up at `/business/join`. The partner picker searches Aim4price account profiles. Standalone external listings and Google listing submissions have been retired.

Use **Share link** to send selected assets to an external recipient. Existing enquiry links continue to use the original permissions, verification, and revocation checks.

Business verification is available under **Accounts → Manage → Business verification**. Admins can review legacy guest report access under **Sharing & access**.
